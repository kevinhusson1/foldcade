# ADR 0002 — Concept: a mix of two references

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-11-24

## Context
Two references inspire the project: a commercial closable arcade cabinet
(storage base with double doors, arcade module that folds onto it) and a
homemade "hidden arcade" video build.

## Decision (draft)
Combine the **furniture-grade look and storage base** of the first with the
**accessible, workshop-level build method** of the second. Redraw everything
from scratch; no copied drawings, artwork or branding.

## To decide before v0.1
Fold mechanism, screen size/ratio, computer platform, material, target budget,
overall dimensions. Each gets its own ADR (0003+).
