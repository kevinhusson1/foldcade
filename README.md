## What is FoldCade?

FoldCade is a fully documented, buildable and **hackable** 2-player arcade
cabinet. Its signature idea: the arcade unit **folds away** and sits on a wooden
storage cabinet, so that when you are done playing, the room just shows a nice
piece of furniture — an arcade machine *hidden in plain sight*.

It is a deliberate **mix of two inspirations**:

1. a **modern, closable cabinet** (stand-up arcade on a storage base with double
   doors, room for a console and cables inside), and
2. a **homemade "hidden arcade" build** that shows how to make such a machine with
   common tools.

The goal is a **clean, original proposal** — not a copy: open plans, an honest
bill of materials and a build guide anyone can follow.

> **Status: concept / pre-v0.1.** Nothing here is validated yet. See the
> [roadmap](docs/roadmap.en.md). Do not build from unreleased plans.

## Goals

- ✅ 2 players, 2 joysticks, 6 buttons each, start/coin buttons
- ✅ Screen, speakers and computer hidden or protected when folded
- ✅ Storage for a console / accessories in the base
- ✅ Buildable with a standard workshop (or CNC / laser cutting)
- ✅ Bilingual documentation (EN / FR), BOM with alternatives and prices
- ✅ Safety-first: mains power, dust, pinch points are documented
